Graph WaveNet traffic forecast on Los Angeles highways

Built by Jiajie Su

A trained Graph WaveNet predicts speeds at 207 highway sensors (METR-LA) from the previous hour of readings. Click a sensor on the map, drag the time slider to watch congestion move, and knock out sensors to see how the forecast holds up.
