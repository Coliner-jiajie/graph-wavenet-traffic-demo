Graph WaveNet traffic forecast on Los Angeles highways

Built by Jiajie Su

A trained Graph WaveNet predicts speeds at 207 highway sensors (METR-LA) from the previous hour of readings. Pick a sensor, choose how far ahead to forecast, and knock out sensors to see how the forecast holds up.

Forecast horizon

With sensors missing, a random share of the 207 sensors send zeros instead of readings for each input window. The model has to fill the gap from the sensors that still report.
